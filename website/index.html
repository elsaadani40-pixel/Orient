A higher standard

THE ORIENT PRINCIPLE

An action is not an outcome. Proof is the product.

ORIENT's ambition is not to sound autonomous. It is to become useful, dependable infrastructure for real work: understand the goal, know its limits, take authorized steps, verify the result and report the truth.
